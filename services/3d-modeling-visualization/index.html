WHAT WE CAN MAKE

A complete space. A considered point of view.

Some projects start with photographs and drawings that need to become a coherent 3D scene. Others arrive with an existing model and need convincing materials, lighting and presentation. I separate those tasks at the brief stage so the scope reflects the work actually needed. My Riyadh-based practice includes interior reconstruction, exhibition visualization and rendered scene composition.

01

Modeling & reconstruction

Build and refine scene geometry from an agreed concept or a combination of photographs, drawings and supplied references.

02

Texturing, materials & rendering

Develop the surface treatment, lighting and final images of a new or supplied scene.

03

Presentation & composition

Choose an overall view, eye-level perspectives and details that explain the project as a complete set.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Can you render a model supplied by another designer?+

Yes. The CCM case is an example of texturing, rendering and composition applied to supplied booth designs and models. Those design alternatives are credited as supplied, not presented as original modeling.

Can you reconstruct an interior from references?+

Yes. Lobby Reconstruction documents my end-to-end reference interpretation, modeling, material treatment and scene composition. The original architecture retains its own authorship.

Is a top view enough to present a space?+

A top view explains layout, while eye-level and detail views explain scale and materials. The right mix depends on what the audience needs to evaluate; the view list should be agreed before final rendering.
