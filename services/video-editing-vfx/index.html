WHAT WE CAN MAKE

Every cut and every layer has a purpose.

A film, a short social edit and a display loop ask different things of the same material. I begin with the message, available footage and destination, then shape the edit and the visual treatment around them. The brief can cover an editorial cut, shot-level effects, compositing or a coordinated set of outputs. Specific effects are scoped after reviewing the source footage.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Can you work with footage that has already been shot?+

Yes. Reviewing the footage first helps establish which editing and compositing treatments are practical and whether additional source material is needed.

Can one edit be adapted for multiple screens?+

Yes, with the formats agreed early. Vertical, landscape and installation screens can require different compositions rather than a simple crop of one master.

Can you quote an effects-heavy project from a short description?+

A description is a useful start, but representative shots and reference treatments are needed to define the effects scope. Footage quality, shot length and the required level of integration all affect the work.
