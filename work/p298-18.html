02 / DESIGN APPROACH

From intention
to experience.

Six icons occupy the selection panel while a matching panel previews the chosen value. Large cancel and continue actions keep the next step distinct.

01

Six symbolic choices

The first artboard shows six symbol choices, a selected creativity explanation and Cancel session/Proceed actions.

02

Matched explanation panel

The native document includes alternate value-explanation states, supporting a selection-and-preview interface rather than a static icon sheet.

03

Large progression controls

The private derivative removes 83 isolated company-header vector paths. Pixel differences are confined to that header area; interface panels, symbols, instructions and decorative stickers remain.
