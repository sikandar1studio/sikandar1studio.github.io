Yellow and green wrap a thick display surround with diagonal transitions at the corners. An angled presentation view explains its depth around the fruit-slicing image.

01

Two-color display surround

The PNG is an exact pixel-preserving export of that saved composite. Its dimensions and existing white surrounding space are retained, without cropping, upscaling or retouching.

02

Diagonal corner transitions

The mockup places a game image inside a thick rectangular surround, shown at a slight angle so its depth is visible.

03

Angled depth presentation

Yellow crosses the top and left edges; green continues across the base and right side, with diagonal transitions connecting the two colors.
