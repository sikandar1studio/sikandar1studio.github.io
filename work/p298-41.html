Four rounded buttons stack under the rainwater heading. A separate home icon and continuous green pattern establish a consistent navigation hierarchy.

01

Four stacked topic buttons

The private derivative removes 83 isolated logo-vector paths. The patterned raster background, native button objects and text remain, with pixel changes restricted to the header.

02

Separate home action

The screen presents a short topic heading above four rounded buttons, followed by a separate circular home icon.

03

Centered text hierarchy

Two choices are generic method labels, while the others lead to groundwater recharge and benefits content.
