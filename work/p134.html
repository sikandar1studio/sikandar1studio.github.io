02 / DESIGN APPROACH

From intention
to experience.

Contrasting fills separate buildings, roads and open space around a central green corridor. District labels and a legend support the top-view plan, with SVG attributes carrying additional terrain information.

01

Contrasting terrain fills

The 1920 by 1080 image presents a complete irregular site boundary, perimeter highways, many pale building footprints and a broad green central corridor.

02

Central green corridor

The existing map export uses contrasting fills to separate building fabric, roads, open space and landmark footprints.

03

District labels and legend

District labels and a legend make the plan readable as both a visual composition and a guide for later scene construction.
