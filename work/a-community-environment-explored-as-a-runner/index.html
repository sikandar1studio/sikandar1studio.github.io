02 / DESIGN APPROACH

From intention
to experience.

The current workflow connects registration and settings on a tablet to a community, bridge and park route on the main display. Coin feedback, a timer, score and results extend the interface around the running scene.

01

Enter at the tablet

The current documented flow groups touch-to-start, registration, character selection and settings on the tablet interface.

02

Run on the shared screen

The main display introduces the chosen runner, then follows a community route with time and score. Camera-based lane control is documented alongside a keyboard demonstration option.

03

Show collection and results

Coin feedback travels visually toward the score counter. Results and a five-row leaderboard remain over the environment at the end of the round.
