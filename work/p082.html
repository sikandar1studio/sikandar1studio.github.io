02 / DESIGN APPROACH

From intention

The source separates alignment and zoom-out rendering from a portrait gallery interface. The gallery provides photo, video and print choices after the processing stages.

01

Separate capture and retrieval

Four native portrait artboards show a video library, a selected-video view and separate photo/video retrieval states. A labeled detail of the original retrieval controls is prepared; the people-containing preview and campaign footer remain outside that view.

02

Paired-image alignment

The renderer interpolates a crop to create a zoom-out segment and constructs an FFmpeg concatenation step with a supplied second clip.
